wAIllet Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1. Introduction

wAIllet ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our browser extension and related services.

2. Self-Custody Wallet

wAIllet is a self-custody wallet. This means:

  • Your private keys are stored locally on your device
  • We do not receive your private keys or seed phrase
  • You are responsible for securing your keys
  • We cannot recover your wallet if you lose your seed phrase

3. Information We Collect

We collect and process information needed to provide the service:

  • On-device data: encrypted wallet data, settings, and chat history in your browser
  • Account identifiers: wallet address and authentication tokens for login
  • Transaction-related data: addresses, transaction parameters, and simulation requests
  • AI interactions: prompts and responses sent to backend services to process requests
  • Service configuration: favorites, policies, and preferences you save in the app

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Process transaction requests
  • Provide AI-powered assistance and risk analysis
  • Improve service quality and reliability
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers needed to run the service, such as cloud hosting, AI processing, blockchain infrastructure, and content delivery providers.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures. However, no Internet transmission method is fully secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

8. Children's Privacy

wAIllet is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@waillet.io.
